In the past, in ancient times, being a doctor was very different from today. Physicians did not have advanced machines, modern hospitals, or synthesized medicines. Instead, ancient healers—such as traditional herbalists, wise elders, or early physicians like Hippocrates in Greece—relied on nature, observation, and spiritual rituals. They used medicinal herbs, roots, and minerals to treat illnesses and ease pain. Medical knowledge was mostly passed down through generations or recorded in ancient handwritten scrolls. Although they lacked modern technology, their dedication to healing and relieving human suffering laid the essential foundation for modern medicine.
